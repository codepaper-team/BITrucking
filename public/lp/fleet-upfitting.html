Questions

FAQ

What fleet sizes do you work with?

From five trucks a year to standing monthly production slots. The program scales; the consistency doesn't change.

Can you match our current body spec?

Usually yes, including units you currently source elsewhere. Send the spec or photos and we'll quote a match, whether that's through our Dakota, PJ, or STI dealer line or a build off our own floor.

How do you handle chassis supply?

We build on chassis you supply or coordinate with your dealer's bailment pool. We'll fit whichever procurement model you already run.

Do you deliver to multiple locations?

Yes, multi-site delivery across the Southeast is part of the program conversation.

What does pricing look like?

Per-unit pricing at volume tiers, locked for the program term. The price on unit thirty is the price we agreed on unit one.
