Questions

FAQ

How long does a custom truck body take?

Depends on the build and current shop load, but we quote a real date up front. Flatbeds off our own floor move fastest since we control the schedule. Tell us your deadline in the quote form; if we can't hit it, we'll say so instead of taking your deposit and going quiet.

Do you work on my chassis?

Almost certainly. We build and install for Ford, Ram, GM, Isuzu, Freightliner, and most medium-duty chassis. Put your chassis in the quote form and we'll confirm.

Steel or aluminum -- which should I get?

For flatbeds we build, steel is the standard, aluminum when payload and corrosion matter more, and stainless is available but priced at a real premium. Service and crane bodies come from Dakota, PJ Truck Beds, and STI in steel or aluminum depending on the line. We'll recommend based on what the truck actually does all day.

Do you deliver outside Georgia?

Yes, we regularly deliver across the Southeast. Delivery cost and timing get included in your quote.

Can you match a body I already run?

Usually, yes. Fleet consistency matters. Send photos and specs of the existing unit and we'll quote a match, whether that means a build off our floor or the right dealer spec.

Ready to Stop Guessing on Lead Times?

Tell us what you need. We'll tell you exactly when it'll be ready, and mean it.
