Questions

FAQ

How long does a custom truck body take?

Depends on the build and current shop load, but we quote a real date up front and hit it. Simple service and flatbed bodies move fastest. Tell us your deadline in the quote form; if we can't hit it, we'll say so instead of taking your deposit and going quiet.

Do you work on my chassis?

Almost certainly. We build for Ford, Ram, GM, Isuzu, Freightliner, and most medium-duty chassis. Put your chassis in the quote form and we'll confirm.

Steel or aluminum -- which should I get?

Aluminum saves weight (payload and fuel) and won't rust; stainless takes more abuse and welds stronger for heavy-duty cycles. We'll recommend based on what the truck actually does all day.

Do you deliver outside Georgia?

Yes, we regularly deliver across the Southeast. Delivery cost and timing get included in your quote.

Can you match a body I already run?

Usually, yes. Fleet consistency matters. Send photos and specs of the existing unit and we'll quote a match.

Ready to Stop Guessing on Lead Times?

Tell us what you need. We'll tell you exactly when it'll be ready, and mean it.
