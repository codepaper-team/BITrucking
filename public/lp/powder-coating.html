FAQ

What size components can you handle?

Our 30-foot oven lets us take on oversized fabricated components and equipment that most regional shops can't accommodate.

Is this all done in-house?

Yes. Coating happens at our own facility in Madison, GA, the same shop where we build our truck bodies. Nothing gets sent out to a third party.

What's the turnaround time?

Typically 48-72 hours, depending on the quantity and size of what you're sending in.

Do you take smaller individual jobs, or only bulk commercial work?

All powder coating business is welcome, whether it's a single part or a full fleet run.

What color and finish options are available?

We work from a wide standard powder catalog. If your color isn't one we stock, we can order it in, which adds to the turnaround.
