Questions

FAQ

Can you spec a body around the crane I need?

Yes. Our crane and mechanics bodies come from STI, who build the Tiger crane and body as one engineered unit. Send us the reach and load you need and we'll spec the right configuration and handle outrigger integration.

Aluminum or stainless for a service body?

Depends on the trade. Dakota's line comes in aluminum, galvanneal steel, or stainless, aluminum if payload and corrosion are your main concerns, stainless if the truck takes daily abuse. We'll help you pick.

Do you do fleet quantities of the same service body?

Yes, consistent, repeatable spec and mounting across a fleet is a specialty. See our fleet program or note quantities in the quote form.

What's the lead time on a service body?

We quote a real date up front once we know the order lead time from Dakota, PJ, or STI plus our mounting schedule, and we hit it. Put your deadline in the form.

Can you install on a chassis I supply?

Yes. Most customers supply the cab & chassis; we spec and install the body in Madison. We can also advise on chassis selection before you buy.
